# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful and welcoming. Be
considerate, communicate constructively, and do not harass, demean, or threaten
others. Disagreement about ideas is fine; personal attacks are not.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it through GitHub's
report abuse channel for the relevant issue, discussion, or account. Do not
publish someone else's private information. Project maintainers will review
reports and may remove content or restrict participation.

This short policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
